# Why the Name KDNA

**KDNA** is the name of the open file format and protocol for expressible judgment and judgment systems.

The four letters originally drew on the metaphor of **“Knowledge DNA”** (no longer used as an expanded acronym because KDNA specifically preserves active, bounded **judgment** rather than static facts): just as DNA encodes structured instructions that copy cleanly and express reliably in different cells, a `.kdna` file encodes structured human thinking, judgment, trade-offs, and taste so it can be kept intact, read by humans, versioned explicitly, and carried across any compatible AI tool.

## Why judgment, not just knowledge

Documents and wikis store facts and explanations. **Judgment** captures what facts alone never decide: what matters most when good goals collide, which trade-offs you prefer, what counts as good taste, what risks require escalation, and where a rule must stop.

## Built to evolve visibly

Good judgment sharpens as you encounter real counterexamples. Every revision to a `.kdna` work creates an explicit new version (`1.0.0` → `1.1.0`) whose changes can be inspected side by side via `kdna diff --format human`, so your thinking grows cleanly instead of silently drifting.
